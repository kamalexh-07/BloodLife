Please read these terms carefully before using BloodLife.

Welcome to BloodLife. By accessing or using our platform, you agree to comply with the following Terms and Conditions. Please read them carefully.

1. Platform Purpose

BloodLife connects voluntary blood donors with individuals or licensed blood banks in need of blood, in compliance with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, and National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) guidelines. We do not collect, store, or distribute blood ourselves.

2. Voluntary Donations Only

All blood donations facilitated through our platform must be voluntary and non-commercial, as mandated by Indian law (Supreme Court, 1996). Buying, selling, or offering payment for blood is strictly prohibited and may result in legal action, including imprisonment and fines.

3. User Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information (e.g., blood group, contact details, location) when registering or searching for donors.
  • Ensure eligibility for donation per NBTC guidelines: age 18-65, weight ≥45 kg, hemoglobin ≥12.5 g/dL, no transmissible diseases (e.g., HIV, hepatitis), and at least 3 months since last donation.
  • Coordinate donations only through licensed blood banks or hospitals to ensure safety and compliance.
  • Report any suspicious activity, such as attempts to buy/sell blood, to support@bloodlife.com.

4. Data Privacy

We collect and process personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). For details, refer to our Privacy Policy.

5. Non-Discrimination

We promote inclusivity and comply with Indian laws. Users must not discriminate based on gender, caste, religion, or other protected categories when using the platform.

6. Limitation of Liability

  • Actions or inactions of donors, recipients, or blood banks.
  • Misuse of the platform, including violations of Indian law.
  • Inaccuracies in user-provided information or outcomes of donation requests.

All donations must be processed through licensed blood banks to ensure safety and legality.

7. Termination of Access

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate access to the platform for users who violate these terms, engage in illegal activities, or misuse the platform.
