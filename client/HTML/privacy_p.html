Privacy Policy

How BloodLife collects, uses and protects your data.

At BloodLife, we prioritize your privacy and comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

  • Data Collection: We collect minimal data (e.g., name, blood group, contact details, location) to connect donors and recipients.
  • Purpose: Data is used solely to facilitate voluntary blood donations and is not shared with third parties except licensed blood banks with your consent.
  • Security: We use SSL encryption and secure servers to protect your data. CAPTCHA is implemented to prevent unauthorized access.
  • User Rights: You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us at support@bloodlife.com.
  • Consent: By using our platform, you consent to data collection as outlined. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Note: We do not sell or misuse your data. For any concerns, reach out via our Contact page.
